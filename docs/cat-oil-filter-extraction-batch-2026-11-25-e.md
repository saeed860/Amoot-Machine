## Filtration family note

CAT's official comparison pages show 272-1788, 275-2604 and 244-4484 as Standard, Advanced and Ultra High Efficiency filters with the same nominal 5.346-inch diameter and closely related lengths. CAT also lists the 1R-0716 → 1R-1808 → 563-8152 progression with a 3500-engine footnote. These are efficiency/application references, not automatic interchange permissions. Confirm exact serial number and arrangement in SIS/Parts Book before sale or installation.
