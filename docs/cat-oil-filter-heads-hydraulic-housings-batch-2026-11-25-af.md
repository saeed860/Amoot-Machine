## Marine application

- `249-0974` is marked `BOTH` because the official CAT compatible-model list explicitly includes `MARINE ENGINE C4.4`. The product page gives dimensions 2.7 × 3.9 × 4.0in.
- All other records remain `UNVERIFIED` pending full compatible-model extraction or exact serial/arrangement checks. A generic engine/generator category is not treated as marine.
- Bases, housings, adapters, retainers and O-rings are recorded as system components, not misrepresented as filter elements.
- No prices or stock were invented. New records use null prices and stock 0; existing commercial values are preserved.
