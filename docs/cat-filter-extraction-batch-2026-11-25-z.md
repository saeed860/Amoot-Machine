## Data-quality controls

- Only 8M-3120 is marked BOTH because CAT explicitly lists MARINE ENGINE D353E/D353C/D353D/D342C/D342/D343 together with land/industrial models. Generic ENGINE or GENERATOR categories were not treated as marine.
- Parts with no compatible-model list are `REFERENCE` / `UNVERIFIED`; they are not advertised as order-confirmed.
- 161-4741 is discontinued and must not be offered without confirming a valid replacement.
- 143-8086 has a title/specification efficiency mismatch (Standard Efficiency title vs Ultra High Efficiency specification); the conflict is retained for verification.
- CAT alternate-parts pages are used as evidence of related/alternate listings only, not proof of interchangeability. Confirm dimensions, media, seals, pressure/flow ratings and serial range before claiming direct replacement.
- No price or inventory was invented. New products use null prices and stock 0; upserts preserve existing price/stock.
- Machine fitment remains subject to serial number and arrangement confirmation.
