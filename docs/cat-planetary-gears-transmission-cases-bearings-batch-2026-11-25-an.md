## Data-quality controls

- Planetary gear tooth count alone does not establish interchangeability; module, pressure angle, helix, pitch diameter, face width, shaft/bore and carrier arrangement must match.
- 569-4225/569-4226 are planetary assemblies for the listed 335/336/340/345 excavator families; 569-4245/569-4246 are separate assemblies for selected excavator/material-handler/forest-product applications.
- 6Y-8037 is an undercarriage track roller frame guide, not a transmission component.
- 081-3967 is a compactor drum seal bearing, not a transmission bearing.
- 1B-6577, 2J-7137, 4D-4324, 5D-6296, 2J-2152 and 8E-7927 are cone/cup bearing components; each cup must be paired with the correct cone series.
- 7T-1664 is a needle roller cage, 1T-1282 is a thrust race, and 9P-7390 is a friction disc; these are not interchangeable bearing units.
- No marine application was inferred; no prices or stock were invented.
