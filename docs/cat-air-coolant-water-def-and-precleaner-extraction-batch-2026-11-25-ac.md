## Families covered

- Primary/secondary engine air filters, air-cleaner groups and precleaners
- Cabin air filters and crankcase ventilation filters
- Coolant conditioner filters and bases
- Water spray and water-pump filters
- Diesel Exhaust Fluid (DEF) filters and aftertreatment filtration

## Marine and fitment policy

- `505-0160` is marked `BOTH` because CAT explicitly lists `MARINE ENGINE` fitment including C32 MARINE, 3512E, C32, 3516E and C18 MARINE.
- Other records remain `UNVERIFIED` until complete compatible-model details are captured. A generic generator or engine listing does not imply marine use.
- Air cleaner groups, precleaner bodies, coolant bases and other assemblies are categorized as system components rather than filter cartridges.
- No prices or stock quantities were invented. New records use null prices and stock 0; existing commercial values are preserved on upsert.
