## Official CAT Parts Store

- **8S-9130 — Suction Screen:** stainless-steel suction screen for filtering contaminants from oil before it is pumped through the engine. CAT specifies 24-mesh wire, 1086-micron filtration efficiency, 150 LPM nominal flow, -20°C to 120°C operating range, 2.984 in diameter, 3.591 in length, In-to-Out flow. CAT lists underground art trucks, transmissions, toolcarriers, graders, track-type tractors, generator sets, wheel loaders and other land applications.
