## Data-quality controls

- 513-7585 is a hydraulic coupler actuator group, not an engine valve.
- 481-2289 is a UHE hydraulic-filter plate; CAT explicitly says UHE hydraulic filters are not approved for transmission use.
- 365-3006 and 367-8577 are 67-degree rotary air-control valves with different body widths (3.93in vs 3.94in); do not assume interchangeability.
- Gasket thickness alone does not establish fitment; verify inside/outside profile, bolt pattern, material and fluid compatibility.
- Timing pins, cables, fixtures and adapters are classified as service tools, not permanent machine components.
- No model or marine fitment was inferred where CAT did not publish it.
- No prices or stock were invented; existing values were preserved on upsert.
