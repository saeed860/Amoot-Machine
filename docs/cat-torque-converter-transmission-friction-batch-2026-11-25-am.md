## Data-quality controls

- Cat Reman torque converters are kept distinct from new torque converter assemblies. Confirm core-return, housing, pump/valve inclusion, clutch type, stall speed and gear ratio.
- 20R-0130 includes housing, pump and valve; other torque converter SKUs may be bare units or include housing only.
- 6Y-5352 is a parking-brake / planetary friction disc, not interchangeable with ordinary clutch discs based on diameter alone.
- Friction discs require matching inner/outer diameter, thickness, spline tooth count, friction material and clutch pack stack height.
- 5P-1948 is a tapered roller cone; 5P-1949 is a bearing cup. They are different components and must be paired by bearing series and race geometry.
- 523-6092 is an exhaust manifold and is classified separately from transmission manifolds.
- No marine fitment was inferred; no prices or inventory were invented.
