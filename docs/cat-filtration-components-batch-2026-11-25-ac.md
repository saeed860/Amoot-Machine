- 8H-7202 is marked BOTH because CAT explicitly lists Marine Auxiliary 3516C/B, 3512C/B and 3508 variants and Marine Engine D353/C175/C32/C18/C12 families.
- 1R-1804 is not inferred to be marine from a model-string fragment alone; only explicit CAT marine categories are accepted.
- 327-0295 is discontinued and must not be offered without a confirmed replacement.
- 391-2159 has a title/specification mismatch (Hydraulic/Transmission Oil Filter title vs Fuel Filters specification); this conflict is preserved.
- Parts such as bases, cases, covers, elbows, switches, plungers, manifolds and gaskets are cataloged as components, not filter elements.
- Where compatible models are not published, records remain `REFERENCE` / `UNVERIFIED`.
- No prices or stock were invented; existing values are preserved on upsert.
- Final machine fitment requires serial-number and arrangement confirmation.
