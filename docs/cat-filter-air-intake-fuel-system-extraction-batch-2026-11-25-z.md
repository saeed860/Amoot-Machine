## Scope and data handling

- Filters and filter elements are separated from housings, bases, covers, gaskets, valves, hoses, air-cleaner assemblies and other support components. Component rows are not described as filter elements.
- `MARINE` / `BOTH` is used only where Cat explicitly lists MARINE ENGINE or MARINE AUXILIARY. A number of parts are land-only; a small number remain `UNVERIFIED` where fitment is not captured.
- Fitment remains subject to exact engine/machine serial number and arrangement confirmation.
- No unsupported cross-reference or interchangeability claim is made.
- Prices are null where not verifiably sourced; stock is zero for newly created catalog rows. Existing prices and stock are preserved during upsert.
- The official Cat product URL is retained as the source URL in catalog records.
