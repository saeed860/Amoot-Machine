## Data-quality controls

- 0R-7851/0R-7848/0R-7849 are Reman 3500 crankshaft dampers; 0R-7852 is a Reman 3600 basic damper. Do not mix these families.
- 10R-4421 and 10R-6317 are Reman Air Compressor Groups; core-return and included parts must be confirmed.
- Compressor capacity is captured at the stated 1250RPM; this is not a guaranteed installed flow at all speeds or pressure.
- 096-6663 and 096-6664 have the same published 95mm OD but are separate parts; confirm hardness, mounting pattern and load rating before substitution.
- 291-1552 is LH and 291-1551 is RH; they are not interchangeable.
- 206-1345 is a cable fir-tree mount, 116-7964 is an instrument-panel mount, and 103-5423 is a seat rubber block; these are not engine mounts.
- No marine fitment was inferred from generic engine or generator references.
- No prices or stock were invented; existing values were preserved on upsert.
